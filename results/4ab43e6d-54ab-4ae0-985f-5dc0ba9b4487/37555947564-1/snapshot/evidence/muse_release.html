The Muse Spark 1.3 release offers 2 models, each with different intelligence, performance, and pricing characteristics. Below is a comparison of the key metrics across the 2 models.

  • For intelligence, the top model of Muse Spark 1.3 is Muse Spark 1.3 (Max) at 48.
  • For output speed, the fastest model is Muse Spark 1.3 (Xhigh) at 219 t/s.
  • For latency, Muse Spark 1.3 (Xhigh) at 45.94s offers the lowest time to first answer token.
  • For pricing, Muse Spark 1.3 (Xhigh) at $1.37 offers the lowest cost per task. Prices vary up to 1.2x across models.
