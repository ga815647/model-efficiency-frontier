For a route to change numbers, an explicit owner-accepted factor scenario and terms acceptance must accompany evidence for every applicability gate: exact alias, version, effort, quantization, region, coding use case, and current terms. The execution context binds the serving provider, plan, route and exact candidate identity, and must equal every evidenced value. Evidence and acceptance carry explicit validity dates; terms version must equal its cited official source version. The current research date must match the evaluation date. Missing, expired, potential, or mismatched evidence keeps API-only cost and CP. Factors never compound: `M = same-workload API-equivalent spend / subscription spend`, `cost_adj = cost_orig / M`, `CP_adj = score / cost_adj`; Contributor always uses ×1. Accepting a factor or alias cannot bypass another gate. Personal data/confidential suitability and terms acceptance are never inferred.
